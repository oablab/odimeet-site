The RØDE Wireless Micro passes too

September 20, 2026 · OdiMeet development notes

A few hours ago the DJI Mic Mini note said RØDE was on the list to test. The list was short, because a RØDE Wireless Micro happened to be on the table. Plug in, flip one setting, record, combine — pass. It is the second compact wireless mic set OdiMeet certifies.

RØDE Wireless Micro (2 TX + 1 RX)
The receiver is a small block that plugs straight into the iPhone's USB‑C port, no cable; each transmitter clips onto one speaker.
Product image from Amazon. The link is an Amazon affiliate link.

The one thing you have to do: set Channel Routing to Split in RØDE Central

Like the DJI, the receiver can either mix both transmitters into one output (Merged) or give each its own channel (Split). OdiMeet needs Split — TX1 on the left channel, TX2 on the right, one clean track per person. The setting lives in RØDE's own RØDE Central app; OdiMeet cannot change it for you, so it is the only step in the whole flow that needs your hands. Set it, unplug and replug the receiver, and OdiMeet's recording screen shows two channels.

One small note: in OdiMeet's input list this receiver reports itself to iOS as "Wireless Micro", with no RØDE in front — that is the receiver's own USB name, not the app misreading it. "Wireless Micro · USBAudio · 2 ch" is what you want to see.

The result

Record, split, combine — exactly the pipeline the DJI Mic Mini uses: two channels on one clock, so alignment is simply zero; each track transcribed on its own; merged by time; every transcript line carries TX1 / TX2, renamable to real names from the menu. Separation between the lavaliers is in the same league as last night's DJI take — a microphone a few centimetres from a mouth, the other person two metres away. That is set by the hardware, not by an algorithm.

Which one to pick

To OdiMeet they are the same: one transmitter per channel, one mode switch in the maker's app (DJI calls it Stereo, RØDE calls it Split). The difference is form. The Wireless Micro's receiver plugs directly into the phone with no cable and the whole kit is smaller — for people whose phone is the only recording device. The DJI Mic Mini's receiver connects over a USB‑C cable, comes with a charging case, and its transmitters run a little longer. Use whichever you already own.

So

OdiMeet now has two certified compact wireless mic sets: the DJI Mic Mini and the RØDE Wireless Micro. If you have either, or are about to buy one, you can use it with confidence: set Stereo / Split in the maker's app (the only setting you need to touch), plug it into your iPhone, pick the third card in New recording — one track per speaker, and the transcript tells you who said what.

Receivers that work the same way (Hollyland Lark, for one) should work too, but we keep the word "certified" for sets we have actually tested on a table. This setup ships with OdiMeet 1.1.

RØDE Wireless Micro 也通過了

2026 年 9 月 20 日 · OdiMeet 開發筆記

幾個小時前那篇寫完 DJI Mic Mini，說 RØDE 在待測清單上。清單很短，因為桌上剛好有一組 RØDE Wireless Micro。接上、切一個設定、錄一段、合併——通過。這是 OdiMeet 認證的第二套迷你無線麥克風。

RØDE Wireless Micro（2 TX + 1 RX）
接收器是直接插在 iPhone USB‑C 孔上的小方塊，沒有線；兩個發射器各夾一位講者。
商品圖片來自 Amazon。連結為 Amazon 推薦連結。

唯一要你動手的一步：在 RØDE Central 把 Channel Routing 切到 Split

和 DJI 一樣，接收器可以把兩個發射器混成一路（Merged）或各佇一個聲道（Split）。OdiMeet 需要 Split——TX1 走左聲道、TX2 走右聲道，每個人一條乾淨的音軌。這個設定在 RØDE 自己的 RØDE Central 手機 app 裡，OdiMeet 改不了它，所以這是整個流程裡唯一需要你操作的地方。切好之後拔插一次接收器，OdiMeet 錄音畫面上就會看到兩個聲道。

一個小提醒：在 OdiMeet 的輸入清單裡，這支接收器對 iOS 回報的名字就是「Wireless Micro」，前面不會有 RØDE——那是接收器自己的 USB 名稱，不是 app 認錯。看到「Wireless Micro · USBAudio · 2 ch」就對了。

結果

錄音、切軌、合併，走的是和 DJI Mic Mini 完全相同的管線：兩個聲道同一個時鐘，對齊直接為 0；各軌分別轉錄；按時間合併；逐字稿每句帶 TX1 / TX2，選單裡可以改成真名。領夾麥克風之間的分離度和昨晚 DJI 那組同一個量級——麥克風離嘴十幾公分、對面的人在兩公尺外，這是硬體決定的，不是演算法。

兩套怎麼選

對 OdiMeet 來說它們一樣：都是一個發射器一個聲道，都只需要在原廠 app 切一次模式（DJI 叫 Stereo，RØDE 叫 Split）。差別在形態：Wireless Micro 的接收器直接插在手機上、不用線、整組更小，適合手機就是唯一錄音裝置的人；DJI Mic Mini 的接收器用 USB‑C 線連手機，有充電盒，發射器續航長一點。你已經有哪一套，就用哪一套。

好了

現在 OdiMeet 有兩套認證的迷你無線麥克風：DJI Mic Mini 和 RØDE Wireless Micro。如果你手上有其中一套，或正準備買，現在可以放心用了：在原廠 app 切到 Stereo／Split（唯一要做的設定），接上 iPhone，新錄音選第三張卡——兩位講者各一條音軌，誰說了什麼，逐字稿直接寫給你。

同原理的接收器（例如 Hollyland Lark）理論上也能用，但「認證」兩個字我們留給真的在桌上測過的。這個模式隨 OdiMeet 1.1 推出。
