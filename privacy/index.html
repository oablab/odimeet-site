Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

The short version

OdiMeet has no servers and no accounts. Your audio never leaves your iPhone. The only data that can leave the device is transcript text, sent to Google LLC (Gemini, a third-party AI service), and only if you set up summaries with your own API key and allow it on the in-app permission screen. We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your data. The App Store privacy label for OdiMeet is "Data Not Collected".

Recordings and transcripts

Audio you record or import is stored in the app's container on your device (and is visible in the Files app). Transcription runs entirely on the device, using Apple's speech frameworks or the optional SenseVoice model you can download. Nothing is uploaded by these features.

Recording with two iPhones (sync recording, Precision Mode)

When you pair two iPhones, they talk to each other directly over Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity — no internet connection and no server is involved. iOS will ask for Local Network permission for this. Pairing is protected by a six-digit code shown on the host phone. When the recording stops, the guest phone sends its audio track to the host phone over that same direct link, so the host can combine the two tracks; the guest keeps its own copy. Alignment and speaker attribution are computed on the host phone.

Speaker separation on one iPhone (optional)

If you download the optional speaker-separation models and turn the feature on, the app estimates who is speaking from voice characteristics. This runs entirely on the device; no audio or text leaves the phone for it.

Optional: AI summaries by a third party (Google Gemini)

OdiMeet can produce meeting notes and summaries with Google Gemini, a third-party AI service operated by Google LLC. This feature is off until you turn it on, and it is the only way any content from your recordings can leave your device.

  • Who receives the data: Google LLC, through the Gemini API (Generative Language API, generativelanguage.googleapis.com), using an API key from your own Google account. The data goes straight from your device to Google. OdiMeet has no server in between and never receives, sees or stores it.
  • What is sent: transcript text — the words that were spoken, speaker labels (for example "Host", "Guest" or the names you entered), the recording title, and the instructions that ask the model for a summary. Audio is never sent. A transcript can contain personal information about you and about the other people you recorded.
  • How it is collected: the text is produced on your device by on-device speech recognition from a recording you made or imported. It is sent only when you use a summary feature: with live summary on, new text roughly every 30 seconds and the last five minutes every five minutes while you record; for the final summary, the whole transcript once, when you tap Generate or when a recording made with live summary on finishes transcribing.
  • What it is used for: only to generate the live notes and the final summary shown to you in the app. OdiMeet does not use it for advertising, analytics, profiling or model training, and does not share it with anyone else.
  • Optional fact-checking: if you also turn on "Fact-check with Google Search" (off by default), specific claims from the transcript are sent to Google as search queries for the final summary. Google stores grounding prompts and results for 30 days for that service.
  • Your permission: before anything is sent, the app shows a permission screen that explains what is sent, who receives it and why, and nothing is sent unless you tap Allow sending transcript text to Google. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings › Gemini (Withdraw), or remove your key; nothing is sent afterwards. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only and is never synced or logged.

How Google protects the data

Google processes the text under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service, the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The level of protection depends on your key:

  • Paid tier (a key whose Google Cloud project has billing enabled): Google does not use your prompts or responses to improve its products, processes them as a data processor under its Data Processing Addendum, and logs them only for a limited period to detect abuse. This provides protection equal to OdiMeet's own commitment: the text is used only to answer your request. Use a paid-tier key for real meetings.
  • Free tier: Google may use the text to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it after it has been disconnected from your account. Google asks that personal or confidential information not be sent on the free tier. The in-app permission screen says this before you agree. (In the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, Google applies the paid-tier data terms to free use as well.)

Optional: model downloads

Downloading the SenseVoice speech model or the speaker-separation models fetches model files from Hugging Face and from the sherpa-onnx project's GitHub releases. Together with Gemini, this is the only network access the app makes. The downloads transfer no data about you or your recordings; each file is verified against a known checksum before use.

Analytics and crash reports

OdiMeet contains no analytics SDKs and collects no usage data. Crash reports are shared with the developer only if you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings, in which case Apple provides them in anonymized form.

Recording consent

Laws about recording conversations vary by jurisdiction. You are responsible for obtaining any consent required where you record. OdiMeet does not market or design features for covert recording.
